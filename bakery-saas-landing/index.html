Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Deseret.

How does the pricing work?

+

Our pricing is straightforward with three tiers: Starter ($19/mo), Growth ($49/mo), and Enterprise ($99/mo). All plans include core features with additional capabilities as you scale. We offer monthly and annual billing options, with a 15% discount for annual payments.

Does Deseret integrate with my existing POS system?

+

Yes! Deseret integrates seamlessly with most popular POS systems including Square, Toast, Clover, and Shopify. Our Growth and Enterprise plans include full POS integration, and our team can help you set up custom integrations if needed.

What kind of support do you offer?

+

All plans include email support. Growth plans add live chat support during business hours, while Enterprise plans include priority support with a dedicated account manager and phone support. We also offer comprehensive documentation and video tutorials.

Is there a free trial available?

+

Absolutely! We offer a 14-day free trial on all plans with no credit card required. You'll have access to all features in your selected plan so you can fully evaluate if Deseret is right for your bakery before committing.

Can Deseret scale with my bakery as we grow?

+

Deseret is designed to grow with your business. You can easily upgrade your plan as your needs evolve, and our Enterprise plan includes custom solutions for multi-location bakeries. We've helped single-location bakeries scale to franchises with dozens of locations.
